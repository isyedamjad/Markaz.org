About Markaz.org

A Sanctuary of Sufi Literature, Scholarship and Spiritual Heritage

A digital heritage initiative dedicated to preserving, documenting and sharing the rich literary legacy of Sufism and Sunni scholarship.

Knowledge · Remembrance · Heritage

Markaz.org is a digital heritage initiative of Hazrat Hamid Raza Foundation, established by Syed Amjad Hussain. It is dedicated to preserving, documenting and making accessible the rich and diverse literary heritage of Sufism and Sunni scholarship.

❧

Bringing scattered knowledge together, so the wisdom of the past can speak to generations yet to come.

The purpose behind Markaz.org
WHY WE EXIST

A heritage worth preserving

For centuries, Sufi saints, scholars and spiritual masters have enriched the Indian subcontinent through books, poetry, manuscripts, articles, sermons and teachings. Much of this invaluable material remains scattered across private collections, libraries, khanqahs, dargahs and historical archives. Markaz.org seeks to bring these treasures together in one evolving digital home.

OUR SPIRITUAL INSPIRATION

A living connection with our scholarly heritage

Markaz.org draws inspiration from the distinguished scholarly and spiritual tradition associated with Aala Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan, Hujjat-ul-Islam Allama Mufti Hamid Raza Khan, Mufti-e-Azam-e-Hind Allama Mufti Mustafa Raza Khan Noori, Hazrat Ibrahim Raza Khan—widely known as Jilani Miyan—and Huzoor Tajush Shariah Allama Mufti Akhtar Raza Khan Qadri Azhari.

As admirers of this illustrious tradition and devoted servants of Huzoor Qaid-e-Millat Allama Maulana Mufti Shah Muhammad Asjad Raza Khan Qadri Razvi Noori (Damat Barakatuhum al-Aaliyah), we aspire to safeguard our intellectual inheritance and strengthen the connection between younger generations and the scholars, saints and spiritual personalities who shaped it.

OUR PURPOSE

Our vision and mission

Our vision

A lasting home for heritage

To build a reliable, accessible and enduring digital repository for Sufi literature and Sunni scholarly heritage—connecting generations through knowledge, remembrance and a shared appreciation of our intellectual traditions.

Our mission

More than digitisation

We aim to encourage reading, promote historical awareness, facilitate scholarly exploration and inspire younger generations to engage meaningfully with the teachings, writings and legacy of Sufi saints and scholars.

In a world where knowledge is increasingly accessed online, our literary and spiritual heritage deserves a carefully maintained place in the digital realm. Markaz.org is an endeavour to make heritage discoverable, preserve valuable writing and create opportunities for future generations to learn from the wisdom of the past.

BE PART OF THE JOURNEY

Join us in preserving our heritage

Markaz.org is a growing collective endeavour. We welcome authentic books, manuscripts, articles, biographies, historical documents, suggestions and scholarly corrections from readers, researchers, scholars, institutions, libraries, khanqahs and all who value this heritage.
