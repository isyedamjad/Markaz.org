Internet Archive

Find official PDF files attached to an Archive.org book record.

Example: archive.org/details/… or just the item identifier.
Only files actually listed as downloadable by Internet Archive will appear. Some books have no PDF, or may be borrow-only / restricted.

Rekhta.org

Open the original book page and use Rekhta’s own download controls, if provided for that title.

Important: This tool does not bypass login, subscription, DRM, viewer restrictions, or download controls. If Rekhta offers a legitimate PDF download for the book, download it from the official page.
A direct link opens in a new tab. Saving may depend on the host’s permissions and your browser.

How to use

  1. For Internet Archive, paste the book’s /details/identifier URL and tap Find available PDFs.
  2. Choose Download PDF for an openly downloadable PDF, or Open file to inspect it first.
  3. For Rekhta, open the book page and use its official download option if available. Not every title is downloadable.
