Sources & bibliography

Guidance for reliable archive records.

Record sources carefully

For books, record author, full title, publisher, edition, year and page numbers. For manuscripts, record collection, shelfmark, folio and repository.

For oral histories, record interview date, place, interviewer, consent status and restrictions. Distinguish primary evidence from later retellings, and mark uncertain dates clearly.

Only publish copyrighted texts, photographs or manuscripts when you have permission or a lawful basis. Keep licence and attribution details with the record.
