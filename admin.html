Research integrity

Sources & bibliography

Source notes are saved with each record in this starter version.

Bibliographic practice

Record enough detail for another researcher to locate the source: author, full title, publisher or archive, edition, publication year, page or folio, stable URL and access date where relevant.

For oral histories, note the interview date, place, interviewer, consent status and any restrictions. Distinguish a primary source from a later retelling. Mark uncertain dates as uncertain rather than presenting an estimate as fact.

Do not upload copyrighted books, photographs or manuscripts unless you have permission or a clear lawful basis. Keep a record of licences and attribution.

Dedicated source-library tables and media uploads can be connected after the base content editor is verified.
